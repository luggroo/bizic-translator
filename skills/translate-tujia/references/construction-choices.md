# Northern Tujia construction choices / 北部土家语常见构式消歧

**Purpose:** This is the `translate-tujia` grammar subskill / prechecked decision table. Read the relevant entry **before** repetitive database searches. These are documented Northern patterns, primarily Brassett, Brassett & Lu (2006), as summarized with examples in Bizic Academy `docs/reference-grammar.md`. Reuse a rule when the lexical predicate class, scope, participants and construction match. Search the database **only** for genuinely new applications, dialectal contradictions or missing examples. Do not treat this as a complete grammar or silently apply Northern forms to Southern.

## 1. Event linkage: liex vs mor vs bor (most common translation trap)

| Intended relation | Use | Attested example | Interpretation / source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One event is completed before the next | **V liex**, `liex` /lie⁵⁵/ is perfective (not a generic 'and') | `wox liex, youf tongv, aixliex laf pev buv` | “洗好后再蒸，接着泡一夜”；Brassett 2006:69, ex. 86. The same construction can describe **future instructions**, not just past tense. |
| Dynamic manner or accompanying action | **[manner phrase] mor + main predicate**, `mor` /mɔ²¹/ | /kuɔ²⁴ ɲie⁵¹ pɔ²¹ mɔ²¹ tsau²¹/ | “他笑着走了”；Brassett 2006:152–154, ex. 32. The phrase describes **how** the walking happened, not completed laughing before walking. |
| Posture/state maintained while acting | **V bor mor** as background manner; `bor` /pɔ²¹/ marks a continuing/resultant state | /ŋa²⁴ ɔ̃²¹ pɔ²¹ mɔ²¹ tsɿ²¹ ka²⁴/ | “我坐着吃饭”；Brassett 2006:152–154, ex. 31. Sitting continues while eating. |
| Continued state alone | **V bor** | `cixpux tongf bor` | “把书打开并保持打开”，Brassett 2006:90, ex. 111 (see reference grammar, imperative/state section). This does **not** automatically mean doing a second action. |
| Both prior completion and manner | **[V liex] ... [V mor]** | /pa⁵⁵ pa⁵⁵ pu²⁴ lie⁵⁵, tɕiu⁵⁵ ta⁵¹ pʰi²¹ mɔ²¹ ka²⁴/ | “打好糍粑后，分着吃”，Brassett 2006:152–154, ex. 25. Different semantic relations coexist in one sentence. |

**Fast decision:** ask (a) “did A finish before B?” → liex; (b) “is A the way B happens?” → mor; (c) “does a resulting/postural state stay true during B?” → bor mor. Bare **V bor** expresses sustained state, not a generic connective. For simple successive clauses, zero overt connector may be preferable. A phrase-level manner, not just a single verb, may be within `mor`'s scope.

**Homograph warning:** `liex` perfective /lie⁵⁵/ is not directional `lier` /lie²¹/. Also `bor` state /pɔ²¹/ must not be confused with the homographic relational/directional `bor` /pɔ⁵⁵/ in other constructions. **Follow tone + grammatical slot, not romanization alone.**

Source pointer: Bizic `docs/reference-grammar.md` §§6.4, 7.3, 4.10; Brassett 2006 §§4.6.3, 9.3.1–9.3.3, 9.6.

## 2. Action completed vs currently relevant: liex vs liaox

- `gaf liex` (“吃过了”, completed fact) versus `gaf liaox` (“已经吃过了，眼下不需再吃”), Brassett 2006:136, exx. 194–195. `liaox` represents completion with current relevance; it is not an arbitrary replacement for `liex`.
- `liao` may be analyzed as `lie` + `gu` by Brassett, but source analyses and actual surface forms are distinct; do not fabricate tone or combine components mechanically.
- **Choice test:** Does the new sentence assert that an event occurred, or does its completed result matter *now*? Mark the scope before selecting.

Source: `docs/reference-grammar.md` §§4.10 and aspect subsections; Brassett 2006:120, 136.

## 3. When a clause is a time setting vs means/sequence

- `nif enrzir zuf, gof songxkox liaox` — “你来时，他已经回家了”; the **background clause ends in zuf** /tsu²⁴/; Brassett 2006:151, ex. 14.
- `angfnix wuf kav zuf, gercif caf` — “我们放牛时玩得很开心”; Brassett 2006:151, ex. 12.
- `zuf` is “when/while (background clause)” rather than automatic “after”. A main event finished before reference time can still take a separate aspect marker; `mor` expresses manner, not simply time setting.

Source: `docs/reference-grammar.md` §6.5.

## 4. Negative statement vs prohibition, and negative scope

- `nif sar lir daf` — “你没有说话”; negative marker follows the predicate; Brassett 2006:90, ex. 116.
- `sar tav lir` — “别说话”; prohibitive `tav` precedes predicate; Brassett 2006:91, ex. 117.
- `nif ger ongrbortaxtixdaf` — “并不是你不能住在这里”; “不能 + 事实否定” encodes **double negation**, not simple inability; Brassett 2006:140, ex. 219.
- **Choice test:** Separate factual negation, prohibition, inability, unwillingness, and negative existence. Never concatenate two independently attested negatives without checking their ordering/scope.

Source: `docs/reference-grammar.md` §§4.7, 4.10.

## 5. Property predicate vs noun modifier vs independent nominal

- `laiv sav` — “今天冷”; bare stative predicate, Brassett 2006:107, ex. 1.
- `gof nier sixbax hxexsex` — “他的衣服脏”; predicate, Brassett 2006:107, ex. 3.
- **Do not** reuse a short predicative property verb as an attributive form automatically. Some nouns take derived adjective forms, other modifiers use clause linkage or nominalization. A head noun is not the same as an independently referring nominalized clause.
- Specifically, Feng (2025), p.10, exx. 27a/b: “昨天下蛋的母鸡” `[puni alie lie-nie] raniga`; “昨天下蛋的那只” uses `[puni ra-lie lie-nie-xi]` without a head noun, with `xi` required in that recorded context. **This source example has no tones; do not invent them.**

Source: `docs/reference-grammar.md` §§4.2, 6.2–6.3.

## 6. Noun predication vs location/existence vs event predicate

- `gof bifzixkar` — “他是土家人”; positive nominal predicate may lack an explicit copula; Brassett 2006:83, ex. 59.
- `cov woftux lov xief` — “屋里有人”; location + participant + existential predicate; Brassett 2006:84, ex. 62.
- Ordinary event SOV order cannot be imposed on an existential or nominal predication; “是”、 “有”、 “在” from the source language need functional disambiguation.

Source: `docs/reference-grammar.md` §§2.1–2.3, 4.8.

## 7. Instrument, lexical borrowing and nominal counting

- `haxcex wox liex, jiux tortor horlier sov` — “菜洗完了以后，用菜刀切”; tool NP comes **before** `horlier` (“用”), the cutting predicate follows; Brassett 2006:153–155, instrument example. Compare with the exact reference grammar entry before altering argument roles.
- `wav qif kuaiv` (“七块瓦”), `zofzix yif zangx` (“一张桌子”) — the attested **noun + numeral + classifier** order survives Chinese loan material; Brassett 2006:97, exx. 41–42.
- A dictionary gloss like Chinese “棵” or “只” is not a licensed universal classifier. Choose the noun's semantic class from attested examples.

Source: `docs/reference-grammar.md` §§6.5, 7.4 and §3.3.

## 8. Aspect and modality: Chinese adverbs often require predicate morphology

**Load [aspect-modality.md](aspect-modality.md)** whenever the source contains **快要、要、马上、正在、已经、还、还没、刚、曾经、过、不再、先、能、会、可以、该、必须、想、不想、愿意、恐怕** or English equivalents. That reference provides a semantic-choice table, source-specific full examples, and ordering constraints; use it before another corpus query.

| Ambiguous Chinese cue | Direct disambiguation, not word-for-word insertion |
| --- | --- |
| 快要／马上／就要 | An imminent start may use V + /xu/, a prospective form may involve stem + /i/, and approaching completion may use V + /tɕi xu/. See the separately attested examples in the reference. |
| 还／仍然 | “还在做” V + /la ɲie/; “还没做” V + /ta ɲie/; “还没做完” V + /tɕi ta ɲie/; “还不必做” has its own complex modal scope. No universal gloss for /ɲie/. |
| 已经／了／过／刚刚 | A completed event (/lie/), current-result relevance (/liau/), reached endpoint (/tɕi/ combinations), past experience (/po la/), and a maintained post-event state (/po xi/ in the cited context) are distinct. |
| 能／会／可以 | General ability, learned skill, bodily strength, sufficient time, and permission use different modal forms and negation patterns. |
| 该／必须／不必／不许／不想 | Distinguish obligation, authorization, prohibition, volition, and factual negation; the aspect/modality template constrains suffix cooccurrence. |

Reuse licensed examples without repeated research, but do not silently fuse different local varieties or treat a pedagogical form as a corpus quotation.

## Applying this subskill

1. Identify predicate class, polarity, semantic roles, the precise event relation, **and any aspect/modality meanings that Chinese or English might express with adverbs or auxiliaries**. Route those cues to `aspect-modality.md` before lexicalizing them as standalone words.
2. Select a matching documented construction above with its source and limits; do not spend another retrieval round proving the same canonical example.
3. Confirm only **new lexemes** and special constraints through the local database. Escalate to corpus construction retrieval for genuinely different predicate classes, competing analyses, complex scope or Southern locality.
4. Reuse this section as the baseline and mark unsupported new generalizations as provisional; never pretend that an analogous example is an exact attestation.
5. Keep translation complete and user-visible updates incremental as required by the main skill.
